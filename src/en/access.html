Accessibility Statement

Yakir Li is committed to making sure everyone, including people with disabilities, can use this site and receive our services.

Statement updated October 23, 2026

Accessibility level

The site was made accessible in accordance with Israel's Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 5773–2013, and Israeli Standard IS 5568 at level AA, which is based on WCAG 2.0. Our testing also covered the additional WCAG 2.1 level AA criteria.

What we have done

  • Full keyboard navigation with a clearly visible focus indicator, and a "Skip to content" link at the top of every page
  • Headings, menus and page regions that screen readers recognize, with language and text direction set on every page
  • Color contrast that meets level AA, and readable fonts
  • Fully responsive layout for phones and small screens; content can be zoomed without loss
  • Text alternatives for images; decorative images are hidden from screen readers
  • Forms with a label for every field, and error and confirmation messages announced by screen readers
  • Screen-reader notice on links that open in a new window
  • Respect for the device setting to reduce motion
  • An accessibility menu (the round button in the corner of the screen): larger text, high contrast, link highlighting, plain font, text spacing and stopping animations. The menu is an added convenience – accessibility itself is built into the site's code

How the site was tested

Every page, in Hebrew and English, at desktop and phone widths, was tested with the automated axe tool against WCAG 2.0 and 2.1 levels A and AA; with manual keyboard testing; and with Lighthouse. Most recent test: October 23, 2026.

Parts that are not fully accessible

  • PDF files – the Day After Shiva booklet and the lecture flyers are not yet accessible under IS 5568 part 2. The lecture descriptions appear as text on the Lectures page, and we will gladly send the booklet in an accessible format or read it to you by phone.
  • External services – the donation page (JGive), the grandparents group sign-up form (Google Forms), Zoom meetings and the memorial wall (Memoriz) are run by other organizations, and we do not control their accessibility. Each of these can also be done by phone or WhatsApp.
  • External articles and videos on the Media and Research pages link to other sites, and not all of them have captions or an accessible version.

Accessibility of services outside the site

We have no offices or walk-in reception. Our services are provided by phone, WhatsApp, email, Zoom and in meetings at various locations. If you need any adjustment for a meeting (venue access, getting there, hearing), let us know in advance and we will arrange it as far as possible.

Found a problem?

If something on the site is not accessible to you, please tell us so we can fix it – and in the meantime we will provide the information or service another way.
