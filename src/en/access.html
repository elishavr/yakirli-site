Accessibility statement

Yakir Li is committed to making this website accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities.

Our approach

This website was built with the aim of meeting the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility) Regulations, 2013, and Israeli Standard IS 5568, which is based on WCAG 2.0 level AA.

What we have done

  • Full keyboard navigation with a clear focus indicator
  • High color contrast and readable fonts
  • Fully responsive on phones and small screens
  • Heading and menu structure recognized by screen readers
  • Text descriptions for images
  • Reduced motion for users who request it in their device settings
  • Forms with clear labels for every field

Found a problem?

If you come across something that isn't accessible, please let us know so we can fix it.
