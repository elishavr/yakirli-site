Who we are

Civilian bereavement is a widespread reality in Israel. Thousands of families live with the loss of a child, teenager or young adult to illness, an accident or other tragic circumstances – a loss that is not always seen, and does not always receive lasting support.

Since 2015, Yakir Li – Civilian Bereavement Support has worked to stand beside families after the shiva and beyond: through personal and group support, remembrance, public awareness and legislation.
