Civilian Bereavement in Numbers

Many people are unaware of how many children and young adults are lost in Israel every year

Every year, more than 1,200 children, teenagers and young adults up to age 24* die in Israel. Loss to illness and tragic circumstances is civilian bereavement. Behind every number is a family: bereaved parents, and grandparents who have lost a grandchild. And there is no single national framework that supports these families over the years.

* According to Israel Central Bureau of Statistics data, in 2024, 1,232 children, teenagers and young adults up to age 24 died in Israel, from all causes of death.

Yakir Li

Who we are

Civilian bereavement is a widespread reality in Israel. Thousands of families live with the loss of a child, teenager or young adult to illness, an accident or other tragic circumstances – a loss that is not always seen, and does not always receive lasting support.

Since 2015, Yakir Li – Civilian Bereavement Support has worked to stand beside families after the shiva and beyond: through personal and group support, remembrance, public awareness and legislation.

Dasi, my daughter, passed away at 19 from cancer. She was optimistic and gave strength to everyone around her. After she died I came to know dozens of families who had lost a child, and I saw how hard it was for them to return to life, especially on the day after shiva. I understood that I could not return to my previous routine. That is how Yakir Li was born.

– Pini Rabinovich, Founder and Chairman of Yakir Li
