They are there for everyone

They sit there to hear every bit of information about their grandchild. They sit there because their family needs them – for the father and mother who lost their child.

Our program sheds light on the pain of grandparents, gives them tailored support and tools, and strengthens their place as the pillars of the family.

We are here so that no one is alone – especially not those who have held the family together for years.

What we offer

Sharing feelings

A unique setting where you can share freely.

Among peers

Addressing the unique issues of losing a grandchild in a peer group.

Permission to feel

Freedom to express feelings without fear.

A place in the inner circle

Recognizing their place in the family's inner circle of loss.

Coping tools

Practical tools and a sense of control in coping with loss.

Strengthening their role

Reinforcing their special role in a multigenerational family.

Why it matters

An unspoken pain

Losing a grandchild is unlike any other loss. The pain is quiet, but deep.

A unique role in the family

They cope with their own grief while carrying the whole family's need for support.

Worth and meaning

The program aims to restore their sense of worth and meaning in the family and community.

What the program includes

Support groups
