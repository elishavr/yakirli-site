About the workshop

A facilitated workshop of about 10 meetings for grandparents who have lost a grandchild. Participants share longing and pain, meet others who share the same fate and gain tools for coping.

Workshops take place in different places across Israel, and dates change. For details about the next workshop, please contact us.
