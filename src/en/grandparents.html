A double loss

  • They experience a quiet, painful loss
  • They are present at the shiva, sometimes without words
  • They are torn between their own pain and supporting their children
  • They need space, language and emotional tools

In 2024–2026* 16 workshops of 10 sessions each were held, with 182 grandparents who lost a grandchild taking part.

* Data as of 30.6.26

About the workshop

A facilitated workshop of about 10 meetings for grandparents who have lost a grandchild. Participants share longing and pain, meet others who share the same fate and gain tools for coping.

The workshop is led by a facilitator who specializes in loss and bereavement in the third age.

Workshops take place in different places across Israel. For details about workshops in your area, please contact us.
