Mortality data – children, teenagers and young adults

The table shows deaths of children, teenagers and young adults up to age 39, by year and age group. It includes all causes of death and does not separately show the number of deaths in circumstances of civilian bereavement. The number of deaths does not by itself indicate how many separate families have experienced loss.
