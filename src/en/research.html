Civilian Bereavement in Numbers

Mortality data – children, teenagers and young adults

Every year, more than 1,200 children, teenagers and young adults in Israel die from illness, accidents or sudden disasters. Each one leaves behind a family that begins to live with the loss. Most of the public is unaware of the scale.

~1,200
children, teenagers and young adults up to age 24 die on average every year – a constant reality, not a passing event
About half
of those who die are babies and toddlers under 4 – the largest group
~7,400
