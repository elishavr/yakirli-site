The Memorial Garden – digital remembrance

  • A digital remembrance project of Yakir Li that lets bereaved families build a heartfelt memorial website.
  • The platform offers tools for uploading photos, videos, stories and memories – so the light of the loved one keeps shining.
  • It connects the family with the wider circle of friends, community and loved ones, and lets everyone share memories.
  • The project is not only digital – it also offers tools for coping with the loss and for connecting with other families.
  • It turns pain into a living, meaningful memory.

Remembrance as part of coping

We believe that preserving the memory of your loved ones and passing it on to future generations is a meaningful part of coping.

Why a garden?

A garden – unlike a wall, a monument or a gravestone – is a living place: things grew there, bloomed, and keep on living. You return to it again and again – not only on memorial days, but whenever the moment comes: on a Shabbat or holiday eve, on a birthday, in the middle of the week.

What a memorial website offers

Preserving memories

Photos, stories, words – and any unique digital material of your loved one, in one place where nothing gets lost.

Connecting generations

Lets children, grandchildren, friends and acquaintances discover sides they may not have known – and add their own.

A digital legacy

A place connecting past and future, with memories kept for generations – on a secure platform accessible from anywhere.

A Light of Remembrance
