About Yakir Li

Yakir Li was founded in 2015 by Pini Rabinovich, father of Dasi Rabinovich, who died of cancer.

Families facing civilian bereavement have no state framework of recognition and support like the one for military bereavement. We are here so that no family facing a civilian loss is left alone.

What is civilian bereavement?

Civilian bereavement is the loss of a child, teenager or young adult to illness, an accident, a sudden disaster or other tragic circumstances.

Unlike military bereavement, civilian bereavement has no permanent state framework of recognition and support. The families' pain often goes unrecognized and unsupported, and they are left on their own.

The term "civilian bereavement" (shechol ezrachi) was introduced into Israeli public discourse by Yakir Li's founder, Pini Rabinovich.

Dasi's story

Yakir Li was founded in memory of Dasi Rabinovich (1977–1996). Dasi was diagnosed with cancer and passed away on the night of Rosh Hashanah 5757 (14.9.1996), at 19, after a long struggle with the illness.

Dasi is remembered for her remarkable optimism and deep compassion for others. Even during her illness, she brought patients' right to a second opinion and to fair medical care onto the public agenda.

Dasi's motto“Some things are easy and some things are hard, but nothing is impossible.”

Dasi's idea of "Circles of Joy" guides everything we do.
