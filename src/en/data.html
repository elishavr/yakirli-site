Civilian Bereavement in Numbers

Mortality data – children, teenagers and young adults

Every year, thousands of children, teenagers and young adults in Israel die from illness, accidents or sudden disasters. Each one leaves behind a family that begins to live with the loss. Most of the public is unaware of the scale.

What the data shows

2,100–2,400
children and young adults under 40 die every year – a constant reality, not a passing event
About a quarter
of those who die are babies and toddlers under 4 – the largest group
12,000+
families joined the circle of loss in the six years shown in the table alone
