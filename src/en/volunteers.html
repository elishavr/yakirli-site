Who is the program for?

The program serves families after a civilian loss: bereaved parents, grandparents who have lost a grandchild, and the communities around them.

Ways to volunteer

Family companions

Accompanying a family in the first weeks and months after the loss.

Community volunteers

Building a support network and meaningful connections in the community.

Professional experts

Offering help in fields such as therapy and financial advice.

Training

  1. 1

    Regular sharing and learning sessions with professionals.
