The role of the volunteer in Yakir Li projects

  • The volunteer is the vital bridge between the bereaved family and the community – arriving at the family on the day after shiva, just as everyone returns to routine.
  • Receives professional training with a social worker who specializes in bereavement and trauma, and learns active listening, emotional support and identifying needs.
  • Provides a lasting presence – regular visits, phone calls, accompaniment to meetings and practical help.
  • Works in several circles: a close family companion ("the personal embrace"), a community volunteer ("the wider embrace"), or a professional expert.
  • Restores the bereaved family's sense that they are not alone, and that the community sees them and cares about them.

Who the program is for

The program is for parents who have lost a child or young adult, for grandparents who have lost a grandchild, and for communities that want to give families space and support.

Ways to volunteer

Family support volunteers

Accompanying a family in the first weeks and months after the loss.

Community volunteers

Building a support network and meaningful connections in the community.

Professionals

Therapy, financial advice and legal advice.
