Volunteers and training

Join the Day After Shiva program and accompany families facing civilian bereavement right after the shiva.

Sign up

Who the program is for

The program is for parents who have lost a child or young adult, for grandparents who have lost a grandchild, and for communities that want to give families space and support.

Ways to volunteer

Family support volunteers

Accompanying a family in the first weeks and months after the loss.

Community volunteers

Building a support network and meaningful connections in the community.

Professional volunteers

Offering help in fields such as therapy and financial advice.

Training

  1. 1

    Professional training

    Learning how to support families and the tools used in this work.

  2. 2

    Ongoing support

    Guidance from coordinators and professionals along the way.

  3. 3

    Sharing and learning

    Regular sharing and learning sessions with professionals.
