Why give?

In Israel, civilian bereavement touches all of us. Many families have lost their dearest ones to illness, accidents or disasters, and the pain stays with them every day.

Yakir Li supports families through emotional support, guidance, community activities and remembrance. Donations make this work possible.

One-time and monthly donations help us expand our work, start new programs and support more families.
