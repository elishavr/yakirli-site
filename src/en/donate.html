Why emotional support is needed from the day after shiva

  • Emotional support for a family that has risen from shiva is not a luxury but a necessity – losing a child is a deep trauma that affects every area of life.
  • Trauma that goes unsupported can weigh on daily functioning and close relationships – which is why early support matters.
  • A family rising from shiva begins to face a new reality – life without the loved one they lost – and this calls for professional tools.
  • On the day after shiva, when everyone returns to routine, is the most important moment for professional emotional support.
  • Early support protects those who remain.
