Privacy Policy

We treat the details you share with us with care and responsibility. This page explains what we collect, why, and what your rights are.

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Who is responsible

This site is operated by Yakir Li – Civilian Bereavement Support (Registered Nonprofit 580616001), P.O. Box 1208, Efrat 9043500, Israel. The organization is responsible for information submitted through the site (the "database controller" under Israel's Privacy Protection Law, 5741–1981).

Privacy questions: info@yakirli.org · phone / WhatsApp +972-50-533-5148.

What we collect

  • Information you choose to give us – through forms, WhatsApp, phone or email: name, phone, email, town, your relationship to the person who died, and the content of your message.
  • Sensitive information – details about your loss or emotional state may be considered especially sensitive. We never require them. Share only what you want to, and you can always call instead.
  • Aggregate visit statistics – how many people visited each page, from what kind of device and which country, using GoatCounter. It uses no cookies, does not store your IP address and does not identify you personally.
  • Actions on the site – clicks on the WhatsApp, phone, email, donation and download buttons are counted as totals only, with no identifying details.

Cookies and browser storage

The site itself uses no advertising or tracking cookies. If you choose settings in the accessibility menu, they are saved in your own browser only and never reach us.

External services the site links to or uses – donations (JGive), the grandparents group sign-up form (Google Forms), Zoom meetings, the memorial wall (Memoriz), WhatsApp and the site font (Google Fonts) – follow their own privacy policies and may set their own cookies.

How we use information

  • To get back to you and provide the support or answer you asked for
  • To coordinate volunteering, training, lectures and activities
  • To send updates – only if you joined our mailing list, always with a way to unsubscribe
  • To understand, from aggregate figures only, which pages help people, and to improve the site
  • To meet our legal obligations

Do you have to give us information?

No. You are not legally required to provide any information. Without contact details we cannot get back to you, but you can always phone us without filling in a form.

Who we share it with

We do not sell information or pass it to commercial parties. Request details go only to the staff, volunteers or professionals supporting you, and only for that purpose.

Information passes through and is stored by service providers that make the site and our work possible: web hosting, our email provider, WhatsApp and the statistics service. Some of them store data on servers outside Israel (for example in the EU or the US), in accordance with Israel's regulations on transferring data abroad.

We will disclose information to an authority only where the law requires it.

Security and retention

We take reasonable measures to protect information, in line with Israel's Privacy Protection (Data Security) Regulations, 5777–2017: access is limited to those who need it, and the information is not used for any other purpose. No security is perfect, so we also keep only what is necessary.

We keep information for as long as it is needed to support and stay in touch with you, or as long as the law requires. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Mailing list

We send email updates only to people who explicitly agreed to receive them, in accordance with section 30A of Israel's Communications Law. Every message includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also ask to be removed by email or WhatsApp.

Your rights

Under the Privacy Protection Law you may ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and ask to be removed from our mailing list. Write to info@yakirli.org or call +972-50-533-5148. We will reply within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Privacy Protection Authority at Israel's Ministry of Justice.

Changes

If we update this policy, the new version will be posted on this page with its date.

Language

This policy is also published in Hebrew. If the versions differ, the Hebrew version prevails.
