Privacy policy

We treat the details you share with us with care and responsibility.

Who we are

This website is operated by Yakir Li – Civilian Bereavement Support, registered nonprofit no. 580616001, P.O. Box 1208, Efrat, Israel.

What we collect

Only what you choose to give us in the website's forms: name, phone, email, town and the content of your message – including details of your loss, if you choose to share them. Nothing is mandatory, and you can also reach us by phone.

How we use it

  • To get back to you and provide the support or response you asked for
  • To coordinate volunteering, lectures and activities
  • To send updates – only if you joined our mailing list, always with an option to unsubscribe

Who we share it with

We never sell or pass information to commercial parties. Your details are shared only with the staff, volunteers or professionals supporting you, and only for that purpose.

Some services on this site are run by third parties with their own privacy policies: donations (JGive), the grandparents' group sign-up form (Google Forms), Zoom meetings (Zoom) and the memorial wall (Memoriz).

Your rights

Under Israel's Protection of Privacy Law, 1981, you may ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Contact info@yakirli.org.
