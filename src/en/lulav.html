A mitzvah and support together

Sukkot is a festival of joy, connection and unity. You are invited to combine the mitzvah with an act of kindness: buy a lulav – and support families who have experienced loss and civilian bereavement.

Yakir Li – Yad VaLev LaShkol HaEzrachi was founded by Pini, the father of Dasi, who died of cancer. We accompany families who have lost a child or grandchild to illness or sudden disaster – moments when families are left alone and hearts break. We are here to say: no more.
