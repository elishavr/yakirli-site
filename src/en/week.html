Civilian Bereavement Awareness Week

26 Adar II – 3 Nisan 57874.4–10.4.2027
Get an update when the program is publishedLight a memorial candle
  • An annual Yakir Li project that aims to raise sensitivity and simple attention toward families who have experienced civilian bereavement.
  • Born of the understanding that civilian bereavement lacks sufficient public recognition – society wants to support, but struggles to find the words and the actions.
  • Includes lesson plans for schools, community activities and meetings that raise awareness.
  • Meant to give civilian bereavement a place in public discourse.
  • Aims to help bereaved families step out of isolation into the open, have their hardship recognized and rebuild their lives.

What we aim to advance during Awareness Week

Public recognition

Giving families a place in public discourse and raising awareness of their reality.

A caring community

Encouraging mutual responsibility and a sense of belonging.

Policy change

Influencing educational, social and legal processes.

Awareness Week programs

Soul, Spirit and Mind

Lectures, conversations and workshops.

Learning materials

Materials on loss and remembrance for communities and schools.

Sharing stories

A campaign in which families share their stories.

Candle lighting in communities

Memorial candles lit in communities across Israel, and digital memorial candles too.

Discussion circles

Gatherings for sharing and listening between families.

Personal and community support initiatives

Initiatives by families and communities for support and connection.

Memorial ceremonies

Ceremonies in memory of the children, teenagers and young adults who are no longer with us.

A Light of Remembrance

We invite anyone who has lost a loved one to light a virtual candle in their memory. The names and lights join a shared memorial wall that honors the lives of our loved ones.
