Civilian Bereavement Awareness Week

26 Adar II – 3 Nisan 57874.4–10.4.2027

Remembrance continues

The 5786 Awareness Week has ended. Our connection with families and our work of remembrance continue.

Every year we hold a week of events, meetings and activities to bring civilian bereavement into the center of public conversation.

Goals of the week

Public recognition

Giving families a place in public discourse and raising awareness of their reality.

A caring community

Encouraging mutual responsibility and a sense of belonging.

Policy change

Influencing educational, social and legal processes.

What happens during the week

Soul, Spirit and Mind

Lectures, conversations and workshops.

Zoom meetings

Online meetings open to all.

Learning materials

Materials on loss and remembrance for communities and schools.

Sharing stories

A campaign in which families share their stories.
