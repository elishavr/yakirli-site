Dasi's Ripples

Pini Rabinovich, Dasi's father and founder of Yakir Li.

Dasi Rabinovich, a brave and inspiring young woman, passed away in 1996 and left us a legacy: "Circles of Joy." She was known for her struggle with cancer and her fight for the right to fair medical care.

From the day I rose from shiva, I set out on a long journey – to raise awareness of Civilian Bereavement and to help families who lost loved ones to illness and tragic disasters. Out of pain, love and mutual responsibility, I founded Yakir Li.

In this lecture, which combines vision and practice, the ideal and the reality, I tell Dasi's story and share the insights she left behind – to improve public awareness and social action for families of Civilian Bereavement.

The Footprints You Left in Time

A journey with Dasi and in her footsteps
A conversation with Bruria Rabinovich, Dasi's mother.

Facing life's different situations – with Dasi's Circles of Joy.

Dasi was a young woman who lived a life of kindness, love of people, joy and faith. Even in her lifetime she was known for her captivating personality, her courageous struggle with cancer, and her fight for justice and fair treatment from the medical establishment.

Years after her passing, Dasi's personality and deeds still live and resonate in the world, with the power to give hope and optimism and to widen the circles of joy within us and in others.
