The Footprints You Left in Time

A journey with Dasi and in her footsteps
A conversation with Bruria Rabinovich, Dasi's mother.

Facing life's different situations – with Dasi's Circles of Joy.

Dasi was a young woman who lived a life of kindness, love of people, joy and faith. Even in her lifetime she was known for her captivating personality, her courageous struggle with cancer, and her fight for justice and fair treatment from the medical establishment.

Years after her passing, Dasi's personality and deeds still live and resonate in the world, with the power to give hope and optimism and to widen the circles of joy within us and in others.
