Bereaved parents

No one can fully understand the depth of your loss, your pain and your grief. But you don't have to go through it alone.

Contact usJoin our Zoom meetings

We are here for you

We offer personal accompaniment and help on several levels – emotional, legal and bureaucratic.

Reaching out commits you to nothing. You can contact us at any stage – in the first weeks, after a year, or years later.

What we offer

Individual therapy

Access to tailored emotional therapy with professionals.

Support groups

Meetings with other parents who have suffered a loss, including on Zoom.

Personal accompaniment

Day After Shiva volunteers accompany the family after the shiva.

Rights and benefits

Help with authorities and institutions.

Rights you should know about

Rights that Yakir Li worked to anchor in law.

  • A death grant for families who lost a child (up to age 18) who received a disability allowance
  • Vocational rehabilitation for parents who lost their job while caring for a child (up to age 18) who received a disability allowance
  • Exemption from ambulance fees for a patient who died in the ambulance or the emergency room
  • Extended retirement age for bereaved parents – the option to keep working for 4 more years
  • Eased service conditions for soldiers whose family member died in circumstances of civilian bereavement
