A personal note

Parents who have experienced loss and bereavement from illness and tragic disasters

No one can fully understand the depth of your loss, your pain and your grief. But you don't have to go through it alone.

Contact us

We are beside you

We offer one-to-one support and help with emotional, legal and administrative matters.

Bereavement has no expiry date. You can contact us at any stage – in the first weeks, a year later or years after your loss.

What family support includes

Individual therapy

After you contact the hotline, we help you find a professional for emotional therapy. Before therapy begins, we will give you the details of the framework and the cost.

Support groups

Meetings with other parents who have suffered a loss.

One-to-one support

Trained volunteers support families after shiva through The Day After Shiva program.

Help with authorities and institutions.
