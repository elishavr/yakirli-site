One line on the registration form

One question – an opening for support

A simple way to reach families facing civilian bereavement.

The question added to the form

"Has the family experienced the loss of a family member in recent years?"

☐ Military bereavement☐ Civilian bereavement

Our call

  • Kindergartens and schools – add the question to annual registration forms
  • HR departments – ask employees during onboarding and updates
  • Health funds and family doctors – include it in routine patient questionnaires

What it makes possible

  • Reaching families facing civilian bereavement
  • A direct connection to sensitive educational staff
  • Referral to The Day After Shiva program
  • A holistic view of the child in the classroom
  • Breaking the isolation of the bereaved parent

One small question – a big step toward a bereaved family that doesn't know how to ask for help.
