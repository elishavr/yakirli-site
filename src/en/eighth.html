A caring community for families facing civilian bereavement

The Day After Shiva accompanies families after a civilian loss with warmth and professionalism: personal and group support, help with rights and benefits, long-term emotional support and, when needed, referrals for financial and legal advice.

Pairs of professionally trained volunteers accompany families in local authorities and communities across Israel.

Our goals

Emotional and practical support

Being there for the family when everyone else has gone back to routine.

Resilience and a sense of support

Strengthening family members' sense of support and emotional resilience.

Rights and benefits

Helping families claim their rights and easing the bureaucratic burden.

What accompaniment looks like

Home visits

Personal meetings in the family's home, at a pace that suits them.

Phone calls

Staying in touch, even when meeting is too much.

Informal meetings

A talk, a coffee, an outing – whatever helps.

Community commitment

Local volunteers who connect the family to its community.
